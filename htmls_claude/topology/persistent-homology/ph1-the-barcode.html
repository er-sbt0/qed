Topological data analysis · persistent homology, executed

The circle was never drawn. Grow one dial and it announces itself.

A cloud of points has no shape — until you fix a scale. Grow a ball of radius ε/2 around each point; whenever two balls meet, connect them; whenever three meet, fill the triangle. Now sweep ε from nothing to everything and every hole in the data is born at one scale and filled at another. Its lifespan is a bar. The long bar is a real feature; the short ones are the sampling. Nothing here is asserted — the Betti numbers are computed from the complex you are looking at, by the persistence algorithm over 𝔽₂, live.

computed, not claimedβ₀ and β₁ are read off a real 𝔽₂ persistence reduction of the whole filtration — not counted by eye, not hard-coded. Edit the cloud and the barcode re-derives.
the long bar is the truthA feature that survives a wide range of scales is structure; one that is born and dies almost immediately is noise. Persistence is exactly the length of the bar.
one object, every scaleThere is no single "right" ε. The barcode holds all of them at once, which is the whole point — shape is a function of scale, and this shows the function.
a loop, not a numberThe longest H₁ bar is traced in green — its birth edge plus a path recovered by search through the edges already present. It is one representative; every homologous loop around the same hole is an equally valid witness, which is why it is drawn as a choice, not a canonical object.
the honest omissionOnly the single longest loop is traced; the shorter bars are left as bars. Rips is used, truncated at triangles — so voids (H₂) are computed away, not displayed.
